Refund Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy describes how payment queries will be reviewed if payment collection is approved and enabled.

Before payment

Payments, if enabled, are voluntary contributions. Check the amount and payment details before confirming.

Incorrect or duplicate payments

Contact us promptly with transaction ID, date and amount if you believe a payment was made in error. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs or full card details.

Review

Requests will be checked against the payment provider transaction record. Any approved refund will be initiated through the original payment route; bank processing times may vary.
